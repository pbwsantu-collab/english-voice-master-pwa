Rules & বাংলা

ACTIVE: Subject + Verb + Object
↓
PASSIVE: Object + be + V3 + by + Subject

§194 Three Rules

  1. Object → Subject
    Active-এর Object → Passive Subject
  2. Subject → Object (+by)
    Active Subject → Passive Object, সাধারণত by
  3. Verb → be + V3
    Past Participle + to be-এর রূপ

বাংলা পরিভাষা

Active / কর্তৃবাচ্য — Subject নিজে কাজ করে
Passive / কর্মবাচ্য — Subject-এর উপর কাজ হয়
Subject=কর্তা · Object=কর্ম · V3=Past Participle
who → by whom · Retained Object · Perfect Continuous = no Passive

Interrogative 3-Step

1.Assertive → 2.Passive → 3.Interrogative
Do you know him? → You know him. → He is known to you. → Is he known to you?
Did he take tea? → He took tea. → Tea was taken by him. → Was tea taken by him?

who (Active) → by whom (Passive)
